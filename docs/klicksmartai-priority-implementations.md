## Sales-to-delivery workflow

Demand generation (Local SEO/ads/approved outbound) → voice and web chat → qualification → booking and reminders → Atomic CRM → economic impact reporting. Use Vapi, Cal.com, Localo, LeadSniperAI, Hermes and Atomic CRM where appropriate; integrations require testing. Assign owner, baseline, target, measurement window and human escalation for each implementation. Track actual close rate, deployment time, delivery cost, net labour capacity recovered and incremental contribution. Do not double-count benefits or bill overlapping tiers. Outcome target: customer retains 60% of validated economic value after all-in cost. See klicksmartai-catalog.md for safeguards.
